Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

English العربية

Bandly respects your privacy strictly. The app runs entirely on your device — we do not run any server that collects your data, and we never see any of your information.

1. About the App

Bandly is a mobile app that helps you manage and monitor your LTE/4G/5G home router. It connects to your router through your local network to display signal, band, and speed data.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data

The app does not collect or transmit any of the following to any server or third party:

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your router password (stays on your device only)
  • Your router's or network's IP address
  • Wi-Fi network name or password
  • MAC address, IMEI, or IMSI
  • Devices connected to your network
  • SMS messages in the router
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, or files

3. Data Stored on Your Device Only

The app stores the following on your device only and never sends it anywhere:

Data TypeStorage LocationPurpose
Router passwordSecure Storage (Keychain / SecureStore)Auto-login to your router
Router name and addressApp storageSaved routers list
Signal history and readingsApp storageChart display
App settings and preferencesApp storagePreserve preferences

You can delete all this data anytime via the "Delete Router" button in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Network Connections

The app connects to the internet only for the following purposes:

  • Local Router: To read and control your router through your home network only. It does not connect to any router outside your network.
  • Speed Test and Latency: The app uses Cloudflare servers to measure internet speed and connection quality. This is optional and only runs when you tap "Run Speed Test".

There are no other connections.

5. Notifications

The app may send local notifications on your device when connection status changes (network loss, signal improvement, etc.). These notifications are fully local and carry no data over the internet.

6. Permissions

The app requests only the following permissions:

PermissionPurpose
InternetConnect to local router and speed test
Network stateVerify router connection
Wi-Fi stateDetect current connection type
NotificationsAlert you on connection changes (optional)

We do not request: camera, location, contacts, microphone, photos, or any other sensitive permission.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any data with any third party. We use no analytics, no ads, no social networks, no SDKs that track you.

8. Data Security

  • Passwords are stored using Keychain (iOS) and SecureStore (Android) — encryption systems provided by the OS itself.
  • All router communication uses HTTPS when the router supports it, otherwise it runs on your local network secured by WPA2/WPA3.
  • The app cannot send your data to any address outside your local network (technical protection enforced in code).

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

10. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not collect any data from you, all your rights (access, deletion, modification, portability) are inherently guaranteed — all your data is on your device, under your full control.
