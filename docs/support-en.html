Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app store my router password?

Yes, but securely. The password is stored in your phone's secure encryption system (Keychain on iOS, SecureStore on Android). It is never sent to any server.

The app can't connect to my router. What should I do?

  • Make sure your phone is connected to your router's Wi-Fi network (not another network).
  • Verify the router address (usually 192.168.8.1 or 192.168.1.1).
  • Check the password — you may have changed it in the browser.
  • Restart the router and try again.

Why isn't 5G showing up?

5G shows only if:

  • Your router supports 5G
  • Your SIM and plan support 5G
  • Your area has 5G coverage
  • The router is set to 5G NSA or Auto mode (not 4G only)

Go to "Bands" then "Advanced" to enable 5G mode.

Speed test results look strange. Why?

The speed test uses Cloudflare servers. Results depend on:

  • Your actual internet speed
  • Your carrier's network state in your area
  • Server state at test time

For accurate results, run the test two or three times.

How do I delete all my data?

From the home screen, tap the router, then scroll to bottom and tap "Delete Router from App". This removes the password and all data related to the router.

Does the app support my router?

Currently supported:

  • ZTE MU5001 (and similar ZTE devices)
  • Huawei (H138, B818, and others)
  • Other models via "Explore Mode"

If your router isn't supported, try "Explore unknown device" from the home screen and send us the report.

The app uses battery. How can I reduce it?

  • Disable "Automatic monitoring" from the Monitor screen if you don't need it.
  • Close the "Aim Assistant" screen when done (it polls every 2 seconds).

Is there a Premium version?

No. The app is complete with all features at one price. No subscriptions, no ads, no hidden features.
